Why Are Mangoes Good for You?

Mangoes contain vitamins and nutrients that can be part of a healthy diet. They are especially known for containing vitamin C and vitamin A. Mangoes also contain fiber and are naturally sweet, making them a popular fruit for snacks and desserts.
