Braised Pork and Eggs (Thit Kho)

A comforting Vietnamese family dish, slowly simmered until the pork is tender and the eggs have soaked up the savory-sweet broth. Serve with warm rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly, cut into large bite-size pieces
  • 6 eggs, hard-boiled and peeled
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 shallots, finely chopped
  • 1 cup coconut water
  • 2 cups water
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Melt the sugar in a heavy pot over medium heat until it turns amber. Carefully stir in a splash of water to make the caramel.
  2. Add the shallots and pork. Stir to coat, then season with fish sauce and black pepper.
  3. Pour in the coconut water and enough water to mostly cover the pork. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook until the pork is tender.
  4. Add the boiled eggs and simmer uncovered for another 20 minutes.
  5. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Serve warm over rice.
