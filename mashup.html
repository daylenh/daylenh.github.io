Creation, Chaos, and AI

A remix of classical imagery and internet memes.

About the Artwork

Old Art.
New Meaning.

This meme mashup combines Michelangelo's The Creation of Adam with modern internet memes, including Burning Elmo and the I, Robot "No" meme, alongside robot imagery.

By mixing classical art with internet humor, the piece explores human creation of artificial intelligence, the fear of losing control, and the possibility of technology turning against its creators.
