This is a poem gifted by my grandson.

In Vietnamese,

the word for missing someone

and remembering them

is the same: nhớ.

Sometimes,

when you ask me over the phone,

Con nhớ me khong?

I flinch, thinking you meant,

Do you remember me?

My grandson, I miss you.

I know I miss you.

But sometimes when I look at you, I don't know who I am missing.

Your face feels familiar.

Your voice feels familiar.

But your name...

It keeps slipping away from me.

I miss you more than I remember you.
