The Plot

Her name was Lan.

She was a mother, a grandmother, and a woman who spent her life caring for her family.

She remembered the warmth of her kitchen, the smell of her cooking, and the traditions of Tết.

She remembered the recipes she made with love.

She remembered that someone was missing.

But she could no longer remember his name.

Bao was her grandson. He visited her, cared for her, and left little notes to help her remember.

Yet when she read his name, it belonged to a stranger.

Her son Minh remembers Bao.

But even he struggled when his mother could no longer recognize someone she loved.

And so, Grandma Lan kept searching through the pieces of a life she could no longer fully remember.

She forgot names. She forgot faces. Sometimes, she forgot the people standing right in front of her.

But the love her family had for her never disappeared.

The Sad Truth About Alzheimer's

Alzheimer's is a disease that slowly damages the brain, affecting memory, thinking, and everyday life.

The heartbreaking truth is that someone may forget the people they love, even when those people never stop loving them.

Families may have to introduce themselves again and again, share the same stories, and grieve memories that are slowly slipping away.

Sometimes, a familiar face becomes a stranger.

Sometimes, a name disappears.

And sometimes, the person you love is still here, but the memories you shared become harder to reach.

Alzheimer's can take away memories, but it does not take away a person's worth or their need for kindness, patience, and care.

So if Grandma forgets your name...

Sit with her anyway.

Tell her the stories again.

Hold her hand.

Because even if she cannot remember you...

You can still remind her that she is loved.

And perhaps that is what remains when memories begin to fade.
