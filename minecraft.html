About the Project

Creativity in
Every Block.

For this project, I built a Chinesedragon inspired by Minecraft. By creating this dragon in Minecraft, I am representing an important part of my Chinese cultural history.

It is an important symbol in Chinese culture because it is associated with power, strength, good fortune, and prosperity. Unlike the Western idea of dragons as dangerous creatures (like in some TV shows), Chinese dragons are viewed as guardian beings connected to nature.
