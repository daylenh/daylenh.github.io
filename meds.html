The Medicine

Medicine Bottle

A small bottle of medicine with a label and instructions.

A handwritten note is tucked beside the bottle.

Oh, this bottle.

My grandson gave this to me.

He told me that the doctors prescribed this.

Supposedly, it is good for my memory and that I should take it every day.

Oh, there is a little note on the side.

Grandma,

Remember to take your medicine every day, okay? It's important that you take care of yourself.

I'll come visit you often. Love you!

- Bao

...Bao?

Who's Bao?

That's the name written on this note.

He sounds like a very thoughtful boy.

He must care about me a lot.

But why can't I remember him?

Have I met him before?

*Ring... Ring...*

What is that sound?

...Is that the phone ringing?

Who is that?
